
# AWS Support Plans Comparison

## Plans
- **Developer Support**
- **Business Support**
- **Enterprise On-Ramp**
- **Enterprise Support**

## Recommended For
| Plan | Recommended For |
|------|----------------|
| Developer | Experimenting or testing in AWS |
| Business | Minimum recommended tier for production workloads |
| Enterprise On-Ramp | Production and/or business-critical workloads |
| Enterprise | Business and/or mission-critical workloads |

## Case Severity / Response Times*
| Severity | Developer | Business | Enterprise On-Ramp | Enterprise |
|----------|-----------|----------|--------------------|-----------|
| General guidance | < 24 hours** | < 24 hours | < 24 hours | < 24 hours |
| System impaired | < 12 hours** | < 12 hours | < 12 hours | < 12 hours |
| Production system impaired | - | < 4 hours | < 4 hours | < 4 hours |
| Production system down | - | < 1 hour | < 1 hour | < 1 hour |
| Business-critical system down | - | - | < 30 minutes | < 15 minutes |
